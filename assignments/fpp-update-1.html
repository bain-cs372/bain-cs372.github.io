Overview

The main purpose of these reports will be to get feedback on your in-progress work, especially what’s changed from your original project proposal which will now start to morph into a “Design Document” (you can just submit that document with any changes you’ve made).

Additionally, depending on your progress, you might submit examples of:

  • Model output: Have you developed new measures for the output? Do you think your model currently provides a good description of the system’s behavior? Why or why not?
  • Questions: What questions do you have about your model?
  • Next steps: Briefly list your next steps for improving the model.
  • Model Analysis: What conclusions can you draw from the model’s output?
  • For 400 level students: What advanced features do you plan to use?

What to submit

  1. Your progress report (in RTF, DOC, DOCX, or PDF format) – a short narrative description of your progress which can include any of the above points if you’d like feedback on them.
  2. Your updated Design Document (in RTF, DOC, DOCX, or PDF format) – your project proposal but updated.
  3. Your in-progress model(s) (NLOGOX file).
