Activity 2. More advanced Extensions

This activity will require a partner (one other student from the class)!

Choose either Option A or Option B.

Option A - Extending a Sample Model

Pick any model in the Sample Models folder of the Models Library to extend except the following:

  • any models in the Art folder
  • any models in the System Dynamics folder

If you choose Fire, DLA, or Segregation, make sure your modifications are substantially different than those in the textbook. This should be a more substantial modification than the modifications we did in class on Thursday (April 16). Make sure that the modified model shows some interesting new emergent behavior (it is okay if you think that emergent behavior might not be representative of the real world).

Also complete a short slideshow presentation with the old model rules in textual pseudo-code and another slide with the modified model rules in textual pseudo-code. Describe how the behavior of the modified model differs from the original and why you made those changes.

Option B - Creating a Cellular Automata

Take a look at the cellular automata (CA) models Life and CA 1D Elementary in Sample Models->Computer Science->Cellular Automata. The first of these shows a simple 2D CA. The second shows a one-dimensional CA with its time evolution displayed in the second dimension. Create your own cellular automaton model.

Decide on a dimension of the CA (if you’re feeling adventurous, you can use NetLogo 3D and explore a 3D CA). Decide on the radius of the CA neighborhood. Decide on either a synchronous or asynchronous update scheme. Give the CA at least three distinct states. Create presentation slides that describe the rules of the CA and some interesting behavior it exhibits as if it was a model you looked at for Exercise 1.

Activity 3. Reflection

Write a short reflection on your experiences with this part of the assignment. Questions to consider in your reflection are: How did you choose which model to create/modify? When you tried to add new emergent behaviors, did you start with these new behaviors in mind and modify rules to achieve that behavior (top-down) or did you modify rule-sets in order to generate new behaviors (bottom-up)? How did you go about communicating your ideas to your partner–using plain-English, pseudocode, diagrams, drawings, NetLogo code, etc.? How (if at all) did this task change your understanding of how to model emergence using the agent-based approach? Are there things you felt were difficult in the past weeks that seem to “flow better” now?
