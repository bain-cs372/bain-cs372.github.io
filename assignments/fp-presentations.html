Requirements

You only have 4 minutes (so that we can get through everyone) so rather than approaching this as a final summative presentation, think of it more like and advertisement for your project. You should answer the following:

  1. What model did you design?
  2. Why did you design it? (make sure to include your driving question)
  3. What behaviors will it allow you to explore and analyze?

Because these presentations will happen during the last week of class, it might be that you have not yet completed your model / analysis / report. If you do not have final results that’s okay. A preliminary report giving us an idea what you have been up to is okay at this stage.
