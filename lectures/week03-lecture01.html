Tuesday - Lecture 3 - Thought Experiments
Date: April 14

Today we’ll follow up on our discussion of the Hereos and Cowards model and also circle back to cellular automata to talk about wider applications of the study of complex systems.

While you may have heard the term thought experiment before, it’s really crucial to the study of these systems. We’ll look at some classical thought experiments, and try to frame our models as such an endeavor. Then we’ll see if we can apply that sort of thinking to the real world by examining some potential experiments in economics.
