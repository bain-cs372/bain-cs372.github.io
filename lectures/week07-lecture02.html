Thursday - Lecture 12 - Networks (cont.)
Date: May 14

Today we’ll continue our discussion of networks. There were two big things I noticed in project proposals:

  1. An underspecified driving question
  2. A plan that is too complex (e.g. too many parameters; too complex cognition)

We’ll use networks as an excuse to talk about these. First will discuss in more detail different types of networks, namely:

  1. Regular Networks
  2. Random Networks
  3. Small-world Networks
  4. Scale-free Networks

We just need a high-level “intuition” for these structures. We’ll use that intuition to revisit our discussion of the Bass model of information diffusion. We’ll start from the simplest (too simple) version of the model and work our way up from there – centering our Driving Question at each stage.
