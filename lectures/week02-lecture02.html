Thursday - Lecture 2 – Advanced Syntax
Date: April 09

Today, we’ll continue our discussion of specific NetLogo syntax and also look at a more sociological model: Heroes and Cowards. Please make sure to do the assigned readings prior to class and also bring your NetLogo programming questions so we can address them together.

Last time we built a model, Conway’s Game of Life using exclusively patches. Patches are really cool agents, but they’re fixed in place throughout the life cycle of the model. If we want to model movement then we need a different kind of agents: turtles! We’ll spend time today manipulating turtles as well as getting used to some more NetLogo syntax, specifically commands and reporters.
