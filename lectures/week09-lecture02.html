Thursday - Lecture 15 - Validation and Sensitivity
Date: May 28

We’ll spend some time reviewing logistics of the final week of the course and then move on to model Validation. While verification was the process of making sure the conceptual model matches the implementation, verification is about making sure the implemented model matches the intended real-world phenomenon. Note that this doesn’t mean perfectly, it’s impossible to perfectly model the real world. Instead it means thinking through in a deliberate manner how the model matches and doesn’t match the real world.
