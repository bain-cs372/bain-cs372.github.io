Tuesday - Lecture 7 - Designing Models
Date: April 28

Today we’re diving into how to design agent-based models. We’ll first quickly review two examples—Schelling’s Segregation Model and Diffusion-Limited Aggregation (DLA)—and chat about the why and how of ABM: that you should always start with the simplest model possible and only add complexity when your question demands it.

Then together, we’ll walk through the design and implementation process of a model on the wolf-sheep predator-prey system from Isle Royale (which will serve as our real-world reference pattern).

At the end of class, we’ll also start discussing the final project details for the course and (depending on time, I may demo a few prior student made models).
