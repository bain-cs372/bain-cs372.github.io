Thursday - Lecture 10 - Intro to Networks
Date: May 07

We’ll cover a little detail about our Final Projects and then move on to talk more about the environments in our ABMs. For now, we’ve mostly been focused on simple patch-based environments and spatial interactions between agents and their environments. What if we wanted to instead model social networks of agents? We’ll talk broadly about some network theory and implement some basic link agents in a NetLogo model.
