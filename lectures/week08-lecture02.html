Thursday - Lecture 13 - Agent Cognition
Date: May 21

Today we’ll talk about two big design choices in your models: grain-size and agent cognition.

Grain size is a topic we’ve mentioned before but we’ll give a basic framework for why an agent might belong to a particular grain-size (micro, meso, macro) depending on your driving question.

Cognition refers to how your agents make decisions. We’ve seen examples of totally random agents (think DLA or Disease) but also some examples of smarter agents that modify their behavior based off their environment. Today we’ll talk about a framework for separating those different behaviors into (somewhat) distinct categories.
