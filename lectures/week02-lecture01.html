Tuesday - Lecture 1 - History Lessons
Date: April 07

Today we’ll talk about the basics of Agent-based modeling and start to explore NetLogo in a little more depth. First we’ll cover a very brief history of agent-based modeling; reaching back to its roots in the study of cellular automata. We’ll contrast it with equation-based modeling and talk about how the affordances of ABM might lend itself to pushing beyond traditional structurations of scientific problems.

We’ll then spend time building a model in NetLogo. Specifically, we’ll work together to build Conway’s Game of Life, an example of a cellular automata with incredibly simple rules leading to beautiful emergent outcomes.
